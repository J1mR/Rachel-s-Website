Requirements & Compliance

When Do You Need a Domestic EPC?

Sellers

Selling a Property

Under UK law, an EPC must be commissioned before marketing your home. The certificate gives buyers an accurate evaluation of energy efficiency, projected running costs, and improvement options.

  • Lodged quickly to prevent marketing delays
  • Valid for 10 years on the government register
  • Digital certificate supplied directly to you and your agent
Landlords

Lettings & MEES Compliance

Privately rented domestic properties must meet Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards (MEES). Properties currently require a minimum rating of Band E before entering into new tenancy agreements.

  • Identifies cost-effective routes to improve low bands
  • Helps landlords avoid substantial statutory penalties
  • Provides clear documentation for letting agents and tenants

The Inspection Process

What Happens During a Domestic Visit?

Assessments typically take between 30 and 60 minutes depending on property size and extension history. The inspection is purely visual and non-invasive, evaluating how effectively the building retains heat and utilizes energy.

Key Inspection Areas

  • Dimensions, external wall construction, and insulation depth
  • Roof space inspection and loft insulation measurements
  • Primary heating source, boiler controls, and hot water storage
  • Window glazing specifications and draught proofing
  • Fixed lighting efficiency and renewable systems (e.g. solar panels)

Helpful Preparation

  • Ensure clear access to the loft hatch and heating boiler
  • Locate hot water cylinders and primary heating controls
  • Keep paperwork ready for recent insulation, extensions, or solar installations
  • Provide copies of building control sign-offs if applicable
