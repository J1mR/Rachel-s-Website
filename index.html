Meet Your Assessor

About Rachel & Energy Solutions South

Energy Solutions South is an independent, accredited energy assessment practice operated by Rachel. We provide objective, government-registered Domestic and Commercial EPCs throughout Hampshire and the South East.

Whether assisting private homeowners, property investors, or commercial landlords navigating compliance, the priority is always clear advice, flexible booking, and dependable 24–48 hour turnaround times.

Accreditation & Credentials

  • Accreditation Scheme: Elmhurst Energy Accredited Assessor
  • Residential: Level 3 Domestic Energy Assessor (DEA)
  • Commercial: Non-Domestic Energy Assessor (NDEA) Level 3 / Level 4
  • Compliance & Safety: Full DBS clearance and official EPC register lodgement rights
  • Coverage & Insurance: Full Professional Indemnity & Public Liability insurance
