Leasing & Sales

Commercial Sales & Leases

An EPC is legally required whenever a commercial premises is built, sold, or let. The certificate must be commissioned prior to marketing the building and made available to prospective buyers or tenants.

  • Covers new leases, lease renewals, and assignments
  • Accompanied by an official statutory Recommendation Report
  • Lodged securely on the central Non-Domestic EPC Register
Regulations

Commercial MEES Regulations

It is unlawful for landlords to let commercial property with an EPC rating lower than Band E, unless a valid statutory exemption applies and has been officially registered.

  • Protects landlords against severe financial enforcement fines
  • Provides clear cost-effective payback advice for improvements
  • Advises on future-proofing for projected legislative tightening

Technical Inspection

What the Assessment Involves

On-Site Survey

Our assessor measures the building footprint, evaluates thermal envelopes, and catalogues primary building services without disrupting operations.

  • Full dimensional survey and floor zoning
  • HVAC, extraction, and air conditioning equipment analysis
  • Commercial lighting types and occupancy control systems
  • Glazing, wall types, and structural insulation checks

SBEM Calculation & Lodgement

Data is processed using approved Simplified Building Energy Model (SBEM) software to establish the Asset Rating and energy carbon footprint.

  • Production of standard A+ to G commercial ratings
  • Actionable recommendations for carbon reduction
  • Instant registration and digital distribution to all stakeholders
